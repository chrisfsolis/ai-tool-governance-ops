# Control Mapping

| Risk driver | Required review | Example controls | Governance objective |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| PII usage | Privacy review | Do not upload customer PII until privacy review approves the use case. | Reduce privacy and regulatory exposure. |
| Customer data usage | Security review | Enable audit logging and validate access controls before customer data use. | Protect customer trust and data confidentiality. |
| Monthly cost over $500 | Finance review | Review monthly spend and assign a budget owner. | Prevent unmanaged AI spend growth. |
| External vendor in production | Vendor risk review | Vendor must pass security review before production use. | Validate third-party security and contractual safeguards. |
| High risk score | Leadership approval | Leadership must approve residual risk before launch. | Ensure risk acceptance is owned at the right level. |
| All approved use | Baseline controls | Use approved enterprise account only; enable audit logging; require human review. | Standardize safe AI adoption. |
